# Canli Capital finance tools

The `canli` server has three main tools: `find_tool` searches every finance tool in plain words,
`describe_tool` gives a tool's arguments, and `run_tool` runs one tool or several in one call.
Results name their source. Figures read from a filing come with the filing, and the quote beside a
figure is the authority. These tools give data and calculations, not investment advice.
